Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring for my first session? +
Bring comfortable athletic clothing, clean gym sneakers, a water bottle, and a small workout towel. Lockers are provided free of charge.
Are group fitness classes included in memberships? +
Yes! Both our Premium and VIP plans include full access to all group workout sessions such as Yoga, HIIT, and Cardio Kickboxing.
Can I pause or upgrade my membership plan? +
Absolutely. You can upgrade your plan or pause your membership for up to 30 days per year directly at our customer desk.
